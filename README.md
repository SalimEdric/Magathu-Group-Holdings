## About the Project

Magathu Group Holdings is a corporate website developed to establish the company's digital presence and provide a central platform for presenting its business activities, services and company information.

Based in Mahikeng, North West, South Africa, the company operates across construction, property maintenance, general supplies, cleaning and other support services.

The website translates the company's business identity into a professional digital experience, allowing prospective clients and stakeholders to discover its services and initiate enquiries.

## Business Services

The website presents six primary service categories:

1. Construction and Civil Works
2. Property Repairs and Finishing
3. Steelwork and Fencing
4. General Supplies and Distribution
5. Cleaning and Grounds Care
6. Catering and Occasions
