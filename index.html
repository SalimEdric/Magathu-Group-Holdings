01 / OUR COMPANY

Many capabilities.
One commitment.

Service that responds to what you need.

Magathu Group Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a general trading and services company based in Lotlhakane Village, Mahikeng. Our work spans construction, property maintenance, general supplies and support services.

We are committed to understanding each client’s requirements, offering competitive pricing and delivering work with professionalism and respect.
